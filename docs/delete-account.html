Delete your TaxVault account

TaxVault, operated by [BUSINESS LEGAL NAME]

In the app

  1. Open TaxVault and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Under Delete Account, tap Permanently Delete My Account and confirm.

If you pay monthly, auto-pay is cancelled first, so you are not charged again.

Without the app

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my TaxVault account". We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and profile (name, email, phone, country)
  • All receipt photos and documents
  • Expenses, income, mileage trips, invoices and imported bank transactions
  • Activity logs, notification device tokens and coupon records

What is kept

Payment records we must keep by law (tax and accounting) are kept only for the period the law requires, then deleted. Razorpay keeps its own payment records under its policies.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Export your records from Settings first if you need them.
