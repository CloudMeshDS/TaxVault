TaxVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

TaxVault ("we", "us") is a mobile app for scanning receipts, tracking expenses, income and mileage, and preparing tax records. It is operated by [BUSINESS LEGAL NAME], [BUSINESS ADDRESS]. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number (optional), country and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Financial records you add: receipt photos and PDFs, expenses, income, mileage trips, client invoices and bank statement lines you import, with the amounts, dates, vendors and tax details in them.
  • Subscription and payment records: your plan, billing period, payment and subscription IDs, amounts and dates. Card, UPI and bank details are entered only in Razorpay's checkout; we never receive or store them.
  • Device token: if you allow notifications, a Firebase Cloud Messaging token so we can send payment reminders to your phone. It is removed when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Usage and security logs: scan counts (to apply plan limits), sign-in and account activity, and technical logs such as IP address and error reports, used to run and protect the service.

We do not collect your location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and we do not show ads.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the app: store your records, read receipts with AI, produce reports, exports and accountant packs.
  • To manage your subscription, take payments and send payment reminders (push notification and email).
  • To send service emails such as sign-in codes and password resets.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your financial records for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run TaxVault for us, each limited to what it needs:

  • Supabase (database, file storage, sign-in, server functions): stores your account and records.
  • Google Gemini API: receipt images you scan are sent to Google to read the vendor, date, amounts and tax.
  • Razorpay: processes payments and monthly auto-debits.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers push notifications.
  • Email providers (Google Workspace/Gmail SMTP and EmailJS): deliver service emails.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to an accountant only when you choose to share an export with them.

4. Storage and security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our providers. Receipt photos are kept in private storage that only your account can access. Our providers may process data outside your country; we rely on their contractual safeguards for those transfers.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and records for as long as your account exists, so you can use them for your tax records. When you delete your account, we delete your account and records straight away, except payment records we must keep by law (for example for tax and accounting), which are kept only as long as required.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: export your records any time from the app (Settings).
  • Correction: edit your profile and records in the app.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete Account, or see how to delete your account.
  • Notifications: turn push notifications off in your phone's settings.

Depending on where you live (for example under India's DPDP Act, the GDPR, the NZ Privacy Act 2020, the Australian Privacy Act 1988, PIPEDA or US state laws) you may have further rights. Contact us to use them.

7. Children

TaxVault is for adults managing their own or their business's finances. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. For significant changes we will tell you in the app or by email.
